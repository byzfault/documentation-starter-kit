## Delivery Process From Nothing · Folk Digital

**Situation:** an agency of around four people with no delivery process, growing to between 15 and 30.

**What I did:**

- Put in version control, peer and code review and two-week sprints
- Moved support from Zendesk to Jira and Confluence
- Chose Kanban for the 24/7 SLA support desk (10+ clients) and Agile for project delivery
- Added an Agile and waterfall hybrid for clients who needed fixed deliverables and payment gates
- Built a Jira-to-Git deployment pipeline: commits and deployments moved tickets through to-do, in progress, staging, client review and live, with auto-generated staging links and status pings

**Record:** full sprints ran for one to two years or more.

## Platform Audits · 2018 to 2020

**Situation:** e-commerce merchants wanted an independent view of their platform. Clients included ME+EM, Kinetico, Big Green Egg and Madison Bikes (FreeWheel).

**What I did:** ran 10 to 15 fixed-fee audits, autonomously, and presented them in person to client leadership. Each report had a scored executive summary and prioritised recommendations with effort estimates, backed by full evidence: architecture, caching, integrations, hosting, database, per-extension code review graded A to F, performance, security and upgrade gaps.

**Where the value was:** tools produced raw metrics. I called the false positives and found the root causes.

- A disabled flat index was behind 3.3-second category pages
- A single module was throwing 36,500 errors
- The ME+EM audit (2020) covered a headless Magento 2 build with Vue, GraphQL and a Laravel middle layer

## Magento 1 to 2 Migration · Brand Alley

**Situation:** a flash-sale retailer with around 2 million customers and 6 million products, re-platforming from Magento 1 to Magento 2. I worked directly with the CTO and tech lead.

**Approach I planned:** treat it as a data-migration problem at scale. Pre-sync all data silently, then run periodic deltas, so there is no big-bang cutover.

**What I did:**

- Proved the approach on EC2 with sanitised data
- Hit the limits of the off-the-shelf migration tool: 12-hour syncs hanging on CPU and memory
- Scaled the instances and reworked the migration code
- Built AWS deployment pipelines with autoscaling and CI/CD, and worked on SSO and payment and shipping integrations

**Record:** the migration was not completed during my contract.
