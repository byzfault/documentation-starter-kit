### How do you handle subject matter experts who are too busy?

**Reality:** senior engineers are always busy, and I do not expect them to do my work for me.

1. **Do the homework first.** Read the code, run the product, understand the basics
2. **Ask specific questions.** Not "explain this" but "I think X works like Y, where am I wrong"
3. **Use their time well.** A focused 30 minutes beats a rambling two hours
4. **Show work in progress.** Give people something to react to
5. **Earn the engagement.** Demonstrate you understand the system and access stops being a problem

## Process Questions

### What's your typical process on a new engagement?

<Steps>

### Assessment (weeks 1 to 2)
- Understand the product, the delivery state and the commercial goals
- Map stakeholders and decision rights
- Audit architecture, pipeline and process
- Agree priorities in writing

### Planning (weeks 2 to 3)
- Target architecture and sequenced route to it
- Delivery cadence, escalation path, definition of done
- Tooling and workflow set up
- Success measures agreed

### Execution (week 3 onward)
- Ship in short cycles with working software early
- Decisions documented as they are made
- Risk raised early, not at the deadline
- Adjust on real feedback

### Handover (ongoing)
- Documentation kept current with the system
- The team able to carry the work without me
- No knowledge concentrated in one head, including mine

</Steps>

---

### How do you measure success?

**Quantitative:**
- Predictable delivery against agreed dates
- Deployment frequency and time to restore
- Defect and rework rates
- Support burden on engineering

**Qualitative:**
- Whether decisions stay made
- Whether bad news arrives early
- Whether the team can operate when I am not in the room

**Philosophy:** if the team ships predictably and the architecture is still defensible in a year, the job was done.

## Questions I Ask You

Before we work together I want to understand:

1. **What is the delivery problem?** Late, unclear, under-resourced, or all three
2. **Who owns the decisions?** And who thinks they do
3. **What is the current state?** Greenfield, legacy, or a migration halfway through
4. **What does success look like?** Measures, goals, timeline
5. **How does the team work?** Remote, async, meeting culture, reporting lines

**Why these matter:** most delivery failures are organisational before they are technical. I would rather find that out in the first conversation than the third month.
