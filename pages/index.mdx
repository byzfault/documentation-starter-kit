## The position

Forward Deployed Engineer is the role I am targeting. It is also the shape of the work I have been doing since 2010: sit with the customer, find out what the problem actually is, scope it, design it, build the first working version, then get it into production and hand it over.

The route here was developer, DevOps engineer, lead engineer, CTO at Folk Digital in the UK, CTO again at Shero Commerce in New York, senior solutions architect at Klevu, then independent consulting. Since November 2025 I have been contracting inside Mastercard's tokenisation platform.

There is a gap in that route. I was primary caregiver for twin boys from October 2023 to October 2025, and returned to contract work in November 2025.

**On code, plainly.** I read and review code and have done for twenty years. I do not hand-write production code today. I ship with agentic tooling: Claude Code CLI, used daily in client delivery and to build the tools in the portfolio below.

## What I own on an engagement

- **Discovery.** Customer workshops and technical discovery, including the part where the stated problem turns out not to be the problem
- **Scoping.** Proposals, estimates, MVP definition, sequencing
- **Architecture.** Integration design across modern and legacy systems, APIs, auth, payments
- **Build.** AI-assisted delivery from prototype to production
- **Stakeholders.** Steering sessions and competing feedback, adjudicated with the reasoning written down
- **Deployment.** CI/CD, release, handover to the team that keeps it

## Selected work

**Mastercard, MDES Manager.** Contract, November 2025 to present. Mastercard's self-service card tokenisation platform, used by banks, issuers and processors to configure card products for Apple Pay, Google Pay and Samsung Pay. I analysed 949 customer support tickets to find where integrators get stuck, then scoped the programme around those failure points. A 379-page estate became a 200-page guide. Twelve reusable patterns were adopted across more than 100 product screens. [Full case study](/mastercard).

**Starknet Foundation.** Contract, September 2022 to October 2023. Sole owner of the developer-facing surface of an Ethereum Layer 2 through its testnet-to-mainnet launch, working alongside core engineers and ZK cryptographers. [Full case study](/starknet).

**Brand Alley.** Senior solutions architect on a replatform, 2019 to 2020. Led the data migration: 6 million products and 2 million customer entities.
