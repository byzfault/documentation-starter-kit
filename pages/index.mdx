## Overview

I help teams adopt AI inside complex technical environments. At Mastercard I rewrote a 379-page product guide with LLM tooling, built the RAG-ready content repository behind a conversational-AI proof of concept, and run LLM-assisted review of 30 to 40 screens every release cycle. I am now scoping AI content-review agents with the MDES design team.

I read and review code. I ship with agentic tooling.

Before that I spent twenty years in enterprise e-commerce, blockchain infrastructure and card payments. I was CTO at two agencies, Folk Digital in London and Shero Commerce in New York. I also worked as a lead developer, senior solutions architect and independent consultant for brands including Wedgwood, Hermès, Hackett London, MADE.COM, Pernod Ricard, Klevu, StarkWare and Mastercard. I built and led engineering teams of 12 to 20+ across onshore and offshore locations.

I am domain-literate in card tokenisation (Mastercard MDES) and in Ethereum Layer 2 (Starknet, through its testnet-to-mainnet transition). I have been active on-chain across Ethereum and Solana since 2017.

**What I am looking for:** AI enablement and adoption lead roles. Remote, permanent. I would also talk about engineering management at a remote product company, documentation and developer education leadership at AI and crypto infrastructure companies, and technical customer success or solutions roles at AI product companies.
