## Overview

I am a technology delivery leader with 20 years across enterprise e-commerce, blockchain infrastructure and card payments. I have been agency CTO twice, and a lead developer, senior solutions architect and independent consultant. Brands include Wedgwood, Hermès, Hackett London, MADE.COM, Pernod Ricard, Klevu, StarkWare and Mastercard.

I built and led engineering teams of 12 to 20+ across onshore and offshore locations. I owned hiring, 1:1s, delivery process from scratch (Jira, Kanban and Agile, CI/CD, SLA support desk), cloud infrastructure, security and performance, pre-sales and client-facing technical strategy. I ran builds to £300k as technical project manager.

I know payments and blockchain well. That covers Mastercard's MDES tokenization platform (card digitization for Apple Pay, Google Pay and Samsung Pay) and Starknet, a ZK-rollup Ethereum Layer 2, through its testnet-to-mainnet transition. I have been active on-chain since 2017 across Ethereum and Solana.

I work with engineers, cryptographers, designers, executives and clients. I use LLM tooling daily (Claude Code, agent skills, RAG content design) and have delivered it inside a regulated tier-1 environment. At Mastercard that was an LLM-driven 380-page rewrite and the RAG-ready content repository behind a conversational-AI proof of concept.

I read and review code. I ship with agentic tooling.

**What I am looking for:** remote, permanent roles in technology delivery, engineering leadership or solutions architecture. I would also talk about documentation leadership at AI and crypto infrastructure companies, and AI enablement roles.
