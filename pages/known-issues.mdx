## Issue #001: Career gap, October 2023 to October 2025

**Status:** Resolved

Two years out of work as primary caregiver for twin boys. I stayed current with fintech, financial markets and AI tooling through the period, and returned to contract work in November 2025 at Mastercard, on MDES Manager.

That is the whole entry. Two years is two years, and the gap is on the record rather than smoothed over.

## Issue #002: I do not hand-write production code

**Status:** Working as intended

I read and review code and have done for twenty years. I do not write production code by hand any more. I ship with Claude Code CLI, and I own the scope, architecture, review and deployment around it.

This is a real constraint and it rules me out of some roles. It is also why I can take a problem from a first conversation to something running in days. Both portfolio tools and this site were built that way, and the source is public.

If your requirement is a senior engineer typing production code all day, I am the wrong hire and we will both know it by the end of the first call.

## Issue #005: I will tell you when the plan does not work

**Status:** Working as intended, not scheduled for a fix

If the date is unachievable, the architecture is wrong, or the scope has quietly doubled, I will say so early, in writing, to the people who can act on it.

Some organisations want someone who absorbs bad news and reports green until the quarter ends. Better to establish that before a contract starts than after.

## Issue #006: Forward Deployed Engineer is a target, not a past title

**Status:** Open

I have not held the FDE title. The work maps closely: discovery, scoping, MVP delivery, stakeholder management and deployment, across twenty years and two CTO roles. The [FAQ](/faq) has the longer answer. If you need someone with the exact title on their CV already, that is not me.
