## Issue #001: Career Gap (2023–2025)

### Description
Planned service interruption between September 2023 and October 2025, roughly 24 months.

### Root cause
Twins. Two of them.

### Impact
- Reduced professional output during the period
- Limited availability for new engagements

### Resolution
**Status:** ✅ Resolved. Mastercard contract, November 2025.

The gap is behind me. In November 2025 I started at Mastercard on MDES Manager, the global card tokenisation platform. That engagement is live and ongoing.

The mitigation during the gap was staying current across payments, crypto and delivery tooling. The resolution is a contract at one of the largest payments companies in the world.

### Why this is arguably a feature

- **Time management.** When the working window is two hours wide, you stop gold-plating
- **Priorities.** No capacity for perfectionism, so the important thing gets done first
- **Boundaries.** Sustainable pace, contained scope, no drama

**Honest assessment:** taking the time was the right call, and I stayed technically current throughout.
