A technical leader who can:

1. **Run AI adoption** for a team: pick use cases that carry real value, set the standards the tools apply, test them on real work, and get people using them
2. **Lead engineers**, having done it as CTO at two agencies with teams of 12 to 20+
3. **Own delivery** for a build with a date, a budget and stakeholders who disagree
4. **Work in payments and blockchain infrastructure**, from card tokenisation to Ethereum Layer 2
5. **Explain it clearly** to engineers, cryptographers, designers, executives and clients

I read and review code. I ship with agentic tooling.

## Prerequisites

You are a good fit if you have:

- A team or product where AI tooling needs to be adopted properly, not just licensed
- Technically complex material, in payments, fintech, AI or developer platforms
- Delivery that needs owning rather than observing
- A remote-first culture
- Appetite for someone who will tell you when the plan does not work

## What the First Six Months Look Like

**Month 1:** inventory of where AI is already used, the use cases worth pursuing, and the stakeholders who will make or break adoption.

**Months 2 to 3:** standards written down, a small set of use cases tested on real work, false positives tracked, and the first people using it day to day.

**Months 4 to 6:** the tooling embedded in the normal workflow, owners named, and the work carrying on without me in the room.
