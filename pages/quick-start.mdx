## What You Get

One person who can do three jobs:

1. **Solutions architect.** I take a commerce or payments problem from requirements to a design the business signs off. Re-platforming, data migration, integrations, vendor selection and independent audits
2. **AI enablement lead.** I turn a team's standards into agent workflows with skills, an orchestrator, guards on facts and graded evals. Then I get people using them
3. **CTO.** I have run engineering at two agencies, with a team of 20+ at the larger one. Hiring, process, pre-sales, client steering and the hard calls

I can explain all of it to engineers, designers, executives and clients.

I read and review code. I ship with agentic tooling.

## Prerequisites

You are a good fit if you have:

- A commerce, retail or payments platform that needs an architect or a technical lead
- A team with written standards and AI tooling that is not yet trusted or used
- A re-platform, migration or agency relationship that needs an independent technical view
- Remote working, or hybrid in London
- Appetite for someone who will tell you when the plan does not work

## What the First Three Months Look Like

**Month 1:** I learn the platform, the team and the standards. You get a written read of where the architecture or the AI adoption stands, with the risks ranked.

**Month 2:** the first design or the first agent workflow, tested on real work, with the results written down.

**Month 3:** owners named, the work running in the normal workflow, and a plan for what comes next.
