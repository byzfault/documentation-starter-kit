## What You Get

A senior technical leader who can:

1. **Own delivery** for a programme with a date, a budget and stakeholders who disagree
2. **Design the architecture** and defend it to engineers and to the board
3. **Run the engineering team**, having done it as CTO at two agencies with up to 20 people
4. **Work fluently in payments and blockchain infrastructure**, from card tokenisation to Ethereum Layer 2
5. **Write it all up** to a standard that gets signed off first time

## Prerequisites

You are a good fit if you have:

- A technically complex product in payments, stablecoin infrastructure, fintech, SaaS or developer tooling
- Delivery that needs owning rather than observing
- A remote-first culture
- Appetite for someone who will tell you when the plan does not work

## What the First Six Months Look Like

**Month 1:** delivery and architecture assessment, stakeholder map, the real state of the programme in writing, priorities agreed.

**Months 2 to 3:** cadence established, decisions documented, the first releases out under the new process, the worst structural risk retired.

**Months 4 to 6:** the team running at a predictable pace, architecture decisions holding, escalation happening early enough to act on, and the work carrying on without me in the room.
