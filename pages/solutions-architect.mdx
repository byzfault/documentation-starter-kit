# Solutions Architect

**Payments, blockchain and enterprise platforms**

Twenty years designing and delivering enterprise technology: developer, DevOps engineer, lead engineer, twice Chief Technology Officer, and Senior Solutions Architect in a dedicated client-facing role. The work is the same each time. Turn a business requirement into an architecture that is scalable, secure and affordable to run, design the integrations that connect it to whatever is already there, and stay trusted by the engineers building it as well as the executives paying for it.

## What I design

- **Enterprise solution design.** Scalability, security and cost, with the trade-offs written down rather than assumed
- **Integration architecture.** Modern systems talking to legacy ones, which is most of the real work
- **APIs and authentication.** REST design and integration, webhooks and event-driven patterns, OAuth and SSO
- **Payments.** Gateway integration with PayPal, Stripe and Worldpay, card tokenisation on Mastercard MDES
- **Cloud and delivery infrastructure.** Cloud ownership, DevOps, CI/CD on GitHub Actions and GitLab CI
- **Technology evaluation.** Choosing the platform, and being able to defend the choice a year later
- **Architecture governance.** The documentation that keeps a decision from being relitigated every quarter

## Current and recent

**Mastercard, MDES Manager.** Contract, November 2025 to present. Technical authority on the developer- and operator-facing surface of Mastercard's self-service card tokenisation platform, used by banks, issuers and processors to configure card products for Apple Pay, Google Pay and Samsung Pay. I analysed 949 customer support tickets to find where integrators get stuck and rebuilt the platform's content architecture around those failure points: 379 pages consolidated to 200, twelve reusable patterns adopted across more than 100 screens. I also built the structured content repository behind a conversational AI proof of concept, benchmarked against comparable deployments at Klarna and Bank of America. [Case study](/mastercard).

**Starknet Foundation.** Contract, September 2022 to October 2023. Sole owner of the developer-facing surface of an Ethereum Layer 2 through its testnet-to-mainnet launch: API documentation, implementation guides and tutorials for a global builder audience, produced through continuous protocol change alongside core engineers and ZK cryptographers. [Case study](/starknet).

Solution architecture, and enterprise platform audits covering architecture, code quality, performance and cost, with remediation plans written for engineering and business stakeholders in the same document.
