## Ramp-up Time

**Productive within:**
- 1 day: any existing delivery or docs toolchain
- 1 week: a new codebase, enough to review architecture and make calls
- 2 to 4 weeks: a genuinely new technical domain, working with the people who know it

I learn fast in technically complex domains. I will not claim instant expertise in something I have never seen.
